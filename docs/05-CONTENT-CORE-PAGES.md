**Meta title:** `About Ozmo Diet Clinic — Our Approach to Nutrition Care`
**Meta description:** `Ozmo Diet Clinic builds personalised nutrition programmes around your body, your health and your kitchen — with tracking, follow-ups and a dietitian who stays involved.`

## Hero

**H1:** We're not here to give you a diet chart.

**Sub:** We're here to change how you eat — and then to stay with you long enough for it to hold.

## Section — Why Ozmo exists

**H2:** The problem with how nutrition is usually delivered

Most people who come to us have had a diet plan before. Sometimes three or four. They can describe them in detail: the boiled vegetables, the "no rice" rule, the green tea, the two weeks of enthusiasm.

What they can't describe is what happened next — because nothing did. The plan ended. Nobody called. The weight came back, and with it the quiet conclusion that *this doesn't work for me.*

It isn't that they lacked discipline. It's that they were handed a document and left alone with it.

Nutrition isn't a document. It's a set of decisions you make three to five times a day, every day, for months — under real conditions, with real food, in a real kitchen, alongside a job and a family and a wedding season. A plan that doesn't survive those conditions was never a plan.

**Pull quote:**
> Ozmo was built around the part everyone else skips: what happens after the plan.

## Section — What we believe

*Five belief blocks. Large numeral, heading, 40–60 words each.*

**01 — Food is not the enemy**
No food is banned by default. Rice isn't the problem. Roti isn't the problem. Quantity, timing, combination and consistency are almost always the problem — and those are fixable without making your dinner table miserable.

**02 — The plan must fit your kitchen**
If your mother cooks one meal for five people, your plan has to work inside that meal. If you eat out four times a week, your plan has to account for it. We build around your life, not around an ideal version of it.

**03 — What gets measured, changes**
Weight, waist, energy, adherence — we track from day one. Not to judge you, but because progress you can see is progress you keep going for. And because a plan that isn't working should be identifiable in week two, not month three.

**04 — Nutrition supports medicine; it doesn't replace it**
We work alongside your doctor. We read your reports to understand you better, not to diagnose you. We will never tell you to stop a medication, and we'll tell you plainly when something needs a doctor rather than a dietitian.

**05 — Consistency beats intensity**
A plan you follow 80% of the time for six months will beat a perfect plan you follow completely for eleven days. Everything we've built — the tracking, the check-ins, the reminders, the score — exists to protect that 80%.

# `/how-it-works` — How It Works

**Meta title:** `How Ozmo Works — From Assessment to Results`
**Meta description:** `See exactly how an Ozmo programme runs: free health assessment, consultation, a personalised plan, daily tracking, scheduled follow-ups and monthly progress reports.`

## Hero
**H1:** How Ozmo works
**Sub:** Six steps, three months, one dietitian who doesn't disappear after step four.

## The six steps — long form
*Each step gets a full-width alternating row: copy one side, a visual/screenshot the other.*

### 01 — The Health Assessment · Free · ~4 minutes
You start online, on your own time. Seven short sections: your basics, your goal, your health background, your lifestyle, what you currently eat, your medical information, and — the one that matters most — what you think is actually getting in your way.

There's no payment and no commitment. Most people finish it in under five minutes.

**What you get:** an instant Ozmo Health Snapshot — your BMI, your activity profile, your key focus areas and a clear recommended next step.

### 02 — Your Health Snapshot · Instant
A short, personalised summary built from your answers. It tells you where you're starting from and what's worth addressing first.

It's a starting point, not a diagnosis — and we say that clearly on the page.

### 03 — The Consultation · `[CONFIRM: 45–60]` minutes
In the clinic or on video. Your dietitian goes through everything: your assessment, your reports, your medications, your routine, your food, your history with dieting, and what you can realistically sustain.

**Bring:** any lab reports from the last six months, a list of medications and supplements, and a rough idea of what you ate yesterday.

### 04 — Your Personalised Plan · Within `[CONFIRM: 24–48]` hours
Your plan appears in your Ozmo dashboard. Not a generic chart — your meals, your quantities, your timings, built around what you eat and what your body needs.

Every meal has alternatives, so a missing ingredient or an unexpected dinner out doesn't derail the day. Where it helps, the plan includes a simple activity or workout guide alongside the food.

### 05 — Daily Tracking · A few seconds a day
Open the dashboard, see today's plan, tick meals off as you eat them. Log your water. Record your weight once a week.

That's the whole daily commitment — under a minute. But it's the difference between a plan you're following and a plan you think you're following, and your dietitian sees exactly the same picture you do.

### 06 — Follow-Ups & Adjustment · `[CONFIRM: fortnightly/monthly]`
Scheduled check-ins, not optional ones. Your plan changes as your body, your season and your schedule change.

Every month you get a progress report: what moved, what didn't, what we're changing, and what the next month looks like.

## Section — What we ask from you
*This section builds trust by being honest about the deal.*

**H2:** What we need from you

**Honesty.** About what you eat, what you drink, what you skip. We can only build around what's real. Nobody here is going to judge you for a Sunday biryani.

**A minute a day.** Log your meals. It's the single strongest predictor of whether a programme works.

**Patience for about three weeks.** Bodies adjust slower than motivation fades. The first three weeks are the hardest, and the ones where most people quit.

**A message when it isn't working.** If the plan is impossible, say so. Impossible plans get rewritten. Silent clients don't get better.

## Getting started
**Do I need to know what's wrong with me before I book?**
No. Plenty of people arrive knowing only that something feels off. The assessment and the consultation exist precisely to figure that out.

**What happens in the first consultation?**
`[CONFIRM: duration]` of questions and conversation — health history, reports, medications, routine, food, sleep, what you've tried before. You'll leave knowing the plan of action.

**Do I need lab reports to start?**
Not required, but helpful. If you have anything from the last six months — CBC, HbA1c, lipid profile, thyroid panel, vitamin D or B12 — bring it. If you don't, your dietitian will tell you whether anything is worth getting done.

**Is the health assessment really free?**
Yes. No payment, no card, no obligation. You get your Snapshot whether or not you book anything.

## Programmes & plans
**How is an Ozmo plan different from a diet chart?**
A diet chart is a document. An Ozmo plan is a document plus alternatives, plus tracking, plus scheduled reviews, plus revisions when it isn't working, plus a dietitian you can message. The document is the smallest part.

**Can I follow the plan if my family cooks one meal for everyone?**
Yes, and we'd rather you did. Plans that require separate cooking fail within a fortnight. We build around the household meal and adjust your portions, timings and additions.

**I'm vegetarian / vegan / Jain / eggetarian. Is that a problem?**
Not at all. Tell us in the assessment and the plan is built accordingly.

**What if I travel a lot or eat out often?**
Then the plan is built for a person who travels and eats out. Both are normal, and both are plannable.

**Can I change my plan if I don't like something?**
Yes. Message your dietitian. A plan you dislike is a plan you'll abandon, so we'd genuinely rather hear about it.

**How often does the plan change?**
Typically at each follow-up — `[CONFIRM: fortnightly/monthly]` — and any time something isn't working.

## The dashboard
**Do I need to download an app?**
No. Ozmo runs in your browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app, without an app store download.

**How long does daily logging take?**
Under a minute. Tick off meals, add water, and record your weight once a week.

**What if I forget to log?**
You'll get a gentle reminder, not a lecture. Missing a day doesn't break anything — a missing fortnight is worth a conversation.

**Can my dietitian see everything I log?**
Yes. That's the point. They see your meals, your water, your weight and your adherence, so follow-ups are based on what actually happened rather than what you remember.

## Health & safety
**Is Ozmo a medical service?**
No. Ozmo provides nutrition and lifestyle guidance from a qualified dietitian. We don't diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions, and we don't replace your doctor.

**I'm on medication. Can I still join?**
Yes, and please tell us exactly what you're taking. We build around your medication and never advise stopping or changing it — that's your doctor's decision.

**Will you tell me what my lab report means?**
We use your reports to build a better nutrition plan and we'll point out anything that looks worth discussing with your doctor. We won't interpret them as a diagnosis — that isn't a dietitian's job.

**Do you prescribe supplements?**
`[CONFIRM]` Suggested: *Where there's a clear reason, your dietitian may suggest a supplement and explain why. We don't sell supplements, so there's no incentive to recommend anything you don't need.*

**Is there anyone you can't work with?**
Some conditions need a medically supervised diet, and in those cases we'll say so and refer you on. Being told "not us" honestly is better than being sold a programme.

## Practical
**How much does it cost?**
See `/programs` for the full breakdown. `[CONFIRM: pricing]`

**Do you offer refunds?**

**Can I pause my programme?**
`[CONFIRM]` Suggested: *Yes — for genuine reasons like illness, surgery or extended travel, we can pause once per programme for up to [X] weeks.*

**What happens when my programme ends?**
You keep access to your reports and history. Most people move to a maintenance plan — lighter, cheaper, monthly — because the goal was never to finish, it was to keep it.

**Is my health data safe?**
Yes. Your data is encrypted, access is restricted to your dietitian and clinic staff who need it, and it is never sold or shared for marketing. You can export or delete your data at any time. See our `Privacy Policy`.

## `/medical-disclaimer` — Medical Disclaimer

**H1:** Medical Disclaimer

**Ozmo provides nutrition and lifestyle guidance. It is not medical care.**
The information, plans, assessments and content provided by Ozmo Diet Clinic are for general nutrition and lifestyle guidance only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

**Ozmo does not replace your doctor.**
Always seek the advice of your physician or another qualified health provider with any questions about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something provided by Ozmo.

**Never change your medication based on our guidance.**
Our dietitians do not prescribe, adjust or discontinue medication. Any change to your medication is a decision for your treating doctor.

**Results vary.**
Nutrition outcomes depend on individual factors including age, sex, medical history, medication, genetics, adherence and lifestyle. Nothing on this site is a guarantee of any particular result. Any journeys or numbers shown reflect individual experiences.

**Our assessment is not a diagnostic tool.**
The Ozmo Health Assessment and the Ozmo Health Snapshot are educational, self-reported summaries. They do not diagnose any condition and should not be treated as a clinical evaluation.

**In an emergency, do not use this website.**
If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your doctor or the nearest hospital immediately.

**Conditions requiring medical supervision.**
Some conditions — including but not limited to eating disorders, advanced kidney or liver disease, uncontrolled diabetes, and certain conditions during pregnancy — require medically supervised nutrition care. Where we believe this applies, we will tell you and refer you appropriately.
