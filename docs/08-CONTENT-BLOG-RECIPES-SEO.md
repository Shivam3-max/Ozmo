## 1. SEO strategy in one page

**The realistic assessment:** Ozmo will not outrank Healthline, HealthifyMe or Practo on head terms like "weight loss diet." Chasing them is a waste of budget. What Ozmo *can* win:

| Where we win | Why | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Local intent** | National sites can't be local | `dietitian in [city]`, `best dietitian near me [area]` |
| **Long-tail specificity** | Big sites write generic; we write exact | `PCOS diet plan for vegetarians in India`, `what to eat after thyroid medication` |
| **Indian food specificity** | Global sites don't know poha | `is poha good for weight loss`, `how many rotis a day to lose weight` |
| **Question intent** | Featured snippets are winnable | `can diabetics eat rice at night` |
| **Comparison & myth-busting** | High engagement, strong shareability | `brown rice vs white rice for diabetes` |

**The three-tier content model:**

```
TIER 1 — Money pages    Condition pages + programme pages (§06, §07)
                        Convert. Every article links up into these.
TIER 2 — Cluster hubs   Big guides that own a topic
                        e.g. "The Complete Indian PCOS Diet Guide"
TIER 3 — Long-tail      Specific questions, 800–1200 words
                        Catch search, build authority, funnel to Tier 1
```

**Internal linking rule (enforce in the CMS):** every Tier-3 article must link to ≥1 Tier-2 hub and ≥1 Tier-1 condition page. Every Tier-2 hub links to its Tier-1 page and ≥5 Tier-3 articles. **No orphan content, ever.**

## 2. Local SEO — the highest-ROI work on the entire project

Do this before writing a single blog post. `[CONFIRM: city/area]`

1. **Google Business Profile** — fully completed: category "Dietitian" + "Nutritionist", real photos of the clinic, services list, hours, booking link, Q&A seeded with real questions
2. **Reviews** — a systematic ask. Build it into the admin: at programme completion, an automated prompt asks satisfied clients for a Google review. *(Never incentivise reviews — it violates Google's policy and it's dishonest.)*
3. **NAP consistency** — identical Name, Address, Phone across the site, GBP, Practo, JustDial, Facebook, Instagram
4. **`LocalBusiness` + `MedicalBusiness` schema** with geo coordinates and opening hours
5. **A location page** if there's more than one clinic, or if targeting neighbouring areas
6. **Local link sources** — local gyms, doctors' clinics, wellness studios, corporate wellness partners, local news

## 5. Article template

```
1. H1 — the question, or the promise
2. The short answer (2–3 sentences, in a highlighted box)   ← wins featured snippets
3. Why it's more complicated than that
4. The detail, in H2/H3 sections
5. What this means for you, practically
6. A table or list (very likely to be lifted into a snippet)
7. Author box — dietitian byline, credentials, date reviewed
8. Related programme CTA
9. Related articles (3)
10. FAQ block (3–5, schema-marked)
```

**Every article requires:**
- **A named author.** Health content without an author is untrustworthy to readers and to Google. Byline: the dietitian, with credentials.
- **A "Reviewed by" line and date.** `Reviewed by [Name], [qualifications] · Last updated [date]`
- **A disclaimer footer** — the standard nutrition-education line
- **Real specificity.** No "eat healthy and exercise." Every article should contain at least one thing the reader didn't know

**Publishing cadence:** 4 articles/month is realistic and sustainable. 40 briefs = 10 months of content. Better to publish 4 excellent pieces than 12 thin ones — thin health content actively harms rankings.

`Weight Loss` · `Diabetes-Friendly` · `PCOS-Friendly` · `High Protein` · `Low Oil` · `Breakfast` · `Snacks` · `One-Pot Meals` · `Festival Swaps`

Title · hero image · badges (veg/non-veg, prep time, servings, calories/serving, key macro) · **Why this works** *(2 lines — the dietitian's note, this is what makes it an Ozmo recipe rather than a generic one)* · ingredients with Indian measures (katori, cup, tbsp, and grams) · method, numbered · nutrition per serving table · **Swaps** *(make it higher protein / lower carb / vegan)* · related programme CTA

## 7. Technical SEO checklist

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Core Web Vitals | LCP < 2.5s · INP < 200ms · CLS < 0.1. Next.js image optimisation, font `display: swap`, preconnect to Google Fonts |
| Mobile | Mobile-first. 70%+ of this traffic will be phones |
| Schema | Per §03 route table |
| Sitemap | Auto-generated, submitted to Search Console |
| `robots.txt` | Disallow `/portal/`, `/admin/`, `/api/`, `/assessment/snapshot/` |
| Canonicals | Self-referencing on every indexable page |
| OG / Twitter cards | Per-page images. Generate condition-page OG images dynamically |
| Breadcrumbs | Visible + `BreadcrumbList` schema |
| Internal search | Site search over blog + recipes + conditions |
| 404 | Helpful — links to conditions, programmes, assessment |
| Analytics | GA4 + Search Console — **firing only on `(marketing)` routes, never on portal or admin** |
| Consent | Cookie banner with genuine reject option; analytics blocked until consent |
| Speed budget | Homepage < 200KB JS. Condition pages should be near-static |
